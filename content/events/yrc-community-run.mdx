Our community runs are the heartbeat of YRC — no bibs, no pressure, no one left behind. Show up, run together, stay for the conversation after.

## What to expect

A relaxed 5K or 10K at conversational pace, grouped so nobody runs alone. First-timers are welcome and looked after from the first step.

- **Distance:** 5K / 10K, your choice on the day
- **Pace:** easy and social — this is not a workout
- **After:** coffee and time to connect with the community

## How to join

Register through the link above so we know to expect you. The exact meeting point is shared with registered runners a few days before the run.
